PAGE 5: Q averaging, coefficients and controls
A sampler engineering pass does not measure return or prove that a learned critic is correct.
What the local average computes
Qavg(s,u) = (1/K) sum_k Q(s, u + sigma * epsilon_k), using a fixed nearby-noise bank. The current bank is antithetic. The unperturbed center is not explicitly added as a separate sample. Averaging queries Q at nearby inputs; it does not require new transitions or a value-network query.
Local smoothing can soften a false narrow peak or a real narrow optimum. It does not automatically fix broad critic scale errors, out-of-distribution extrapolation, or Q truth. No causal averaging gain, safe repair, or DFlow advantage has been established by these selected-setting outcomes.
