8. WHAT Q AVERAGING, NOISE AND LAMBDA ACTUALLY MEAN

RawQ is the minimum of the two frozen learned IQL target heads, not an oracle.
The actor and IQL Q/V stay fixed. Nearby-action averaging adds no dataset
transitions and trains no new critic. For ONE flow endpoint a=F8(s,z), query
K nearby actions a_k=clip(a+sigma*epsilon_k) and use
 Qbar(s,a)=(1/K)*sum_k min_head Q_target_head(s,a_k), with its action gradient.
These are K critic queries around one endpoint, NOT K flow trajectories.
Sigma is neighborhood width per coordinate before clipping; K is sample
count. The bank stays fixed throughout one action generation and its I
updates. K4/K16 antithetic banks are not guaranteed nested.

Identity-DF schematic (inspect exact frozen source for all clipping/details):
 a_i=F8(s,z_i)
 z_(i+1)=z_i+eta*(kappa*grad_a Q_or_Qbar(s,a_i)-lambda*z_i)
 plus sqrt(2*eta*T)*xi_i for stochastic mode, T1.
Replacing the endpoint Jacobian by identity is a surrogate gradient. Do not
assert a generic exact Boltzmann target for it. Regular mode still has an
initial Gaussian source z0; conditional deterministic updates do not mean
no initial randomness. Lambda1 is latent prior pull, not an L1 norm, data
support detector or Q-error correction. QGF keeps its faithful per-time-slice
clean-action proxy/update; dropping Euler concerned DF endpoints only.

M=flow pseudo-time slices; I=DF latent optimization updates; N=particle
candidates; K=Q neighbors; environment steps and episodes are other counts.
Q averaging differs from source stochasticity and paper Jacobian smoothing.
It can soften a narrow spurious peak IF neighbors are better behaved, but
cannot guarantee correction of broad wrong unseen-action Q; it can also wash
out a narrow genuinely good region. Small/zero/negative effects are possible.
Current experiments do not establish a systematic success gain or repairedQ.
Higher kappa or eta need not improve success monotonically; excessive
guidance can saturate or exploit critic error. Same numeric kappa is not
same total guidance. Keep full low-to-high grids and matched comparisons.

Learned-V Bellman residual is self-consistency, not trueQ error. A MonteCarlo
Qpi-reference discrepancy is not automatically true IQL Q error either.
