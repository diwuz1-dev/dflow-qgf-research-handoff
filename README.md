## What the results establish

- Task2 choices are frozen; QGF and FK each achieved 40/40 fresh confirmation successes on all three trained seeds. Current evidence does not establish tuned D-Flow dominance or a causal benefit from Q averaging.
- IPG remains numerically unqualified. The user's strict **all-33** requirement remains; new main heldout tests have **not** started.
- CubeTriple Task3 BC50k and goal-IQL50k training completed; the official BC20 pilot recorded **0/20 successes**. Guided Cube D-Flow/QGF native comparison remains unrun and unqualified.
- Task5 and synthetic D8/D32/D128 failures are retained. Engineering checks and training scores are not native success results or proof of true critic-error repair.

A complete artifact archive **does not close the scientific study** or turn unrun proposals into results. See the dated PDFs for scope and limitations.

## Where QGF fails

In the paper's [Appendix K, Table 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11087v2), QGF scores **0.0 ± 0.0%** on CubeQuadruple tasks 3–5 and **5.3 ± 4.0%** on task 2. Every listed method scores zero on tasks 4 and 5; these are difficult benchmark results, not evidence that D-Flow wins.

In our **PointMaze Task5** fresh confirmation runs, tuned QGF scores **40/40, 36/40 and 40/40** for trained seeds 11, 23 and 37: four failures for seed 23. On our **Task2**, tuned QGF scores 40/40 for all three seeds. These are confirmation results, not new main heldout tests. No tuned D-Flow advantage on these hard cases has been established.
